## 2.0.0 (2026-10-06)

The release where every number on the site comes from our own measurements.

### Features

* **creator pages**: every creator has a page with what we measured on OnlyFans — price and offers now, likes growth over time, posting rhythm, price per new photo or video, promotion history and the creator's place in each category. Every figure carries the date of the check behind it.
* **reviews**: visitors can rate and review a creator. Every review is read before it is published, and paid placements never affect reviews. Creators who verify their page can reply.
* **creator claims**: a creator proves a page is theirs with a code in their OnlyFans bio, then gets a weekly report on Telegram.
* **compare**: up to three creators side by side — price, offers, growth, activity.
* **market reports**: daily OnlyFans market data at [searchonly.me/reports](https://searchonly.me/reports) — the fastest-growing creators overall and in 245 categories, a market overview, a monthly index and the methodology, with RSS and Atom feeds.
* **free tools**: income calculator, pricing calculator, tip menu builder with live shareable menus, and a bio generator.
* **browser extension**: FanPanel for Chrome and Firefox.
* **blog**: guides on finding creators, niches, pricing and safety.
* **quiz**: four questions to a list of matching creators.
* **discover**: 15 category hubs with live counts and prices for every option.

### Improvements

* **speed**: a creator page opens with its name, photo and figures at once while the measured history streams in; search shows a full first page and reads a typed query while you type.
* **search index**: profile updates are batched, so searches stay fast while profiles refresh.
* **accessibility**: WCAG AAA contrast, keyboard access everywhere, light and dark themes checked on every page.
* **privacy**: analytics load only after consent; we never sell personal data.

### Data

* 2,396,509 public creator profiles, 677,812 of them free to follow, from 197 countries.
* 687 filter options in 15 categories.
* Profiles are re-checked continuously; likes, prices and offers are recorded every day.

## 1.0.0 (2026-01-17)

### Features

* **search**: AI-powered OnlyFans creator search with 2M+ profiles
* **filters**: Advanced filtering by ethnicity, body type, age, price, and more
* **photo-search**: Upload a photo to find similar creators using AI
* **near-me**: Location-based creator discovery
* **i18n**: Full internationalization support (EN, ES, PT, FR, DE, JA, KO)
* **seo**: Optimized meta titles and descriptions for all pages
